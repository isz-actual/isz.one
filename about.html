About Ice Station Zebra

Custom coins, pins, and patches for people who earned something and want it marked properly.

The Company

Ice Station Zebra was founded on January 3, 2022, in the Pittsburgh area. We design and make custom challenge coins, lapel pins, and patches for Lodges, units, departments, and fraternal organizations.

A challenge coin marks something real. A degree conferred, a command completed, a year of service, an anniversary of the Lodge. We treat that symbolism with care and get it right the first time.

What We Believe

Built for the people who earned it

Every piece we make marks a real milestone, office, or service. That is the only reason to make one.

Fluent in the tradition

The square and compasses, a unit crest, a department badge. Each carries history and rules of its own. We know them, and we handle them correctly.

One price, everything included

Art, design, mold, and proof are part of every quote. You see one per-piece price, and shipping on its own line. No surprise fees.

Made to be kept

Limited runs, numbered pieces, and retired molds. A coin from us is meant to sit on a desk or in a case for decades.
